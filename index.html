2. Überblick

Du kannst alle Rezepte in Cocktail Rezepte offline nutzen. Wir selbst erheben, speichern und übertragen keine personenbezogenen Daten. Wenn du Rezepte als Favoriten markierst, wird diese Auswahl ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Du kannst deine Einwilligung jederzeit mit Wirkung für die Zukunft widerrufen, indem du uns eine E-Mail an die oben genannte Adresse schickst. 2. Overview

You can use all recipes in Cocktail Recipes offline. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data ourselves. The exception is advertising (see section 5): when you are connected to the internet, the app loads ad banners from Google AdMob. Google processes data in the process.

3. Data stored on your device

If you mark recipes as favorites, this selection is stored only locally on your device. The same applies to your language setting, your ingredient selection under “What can I mix?” and your age confirmation. We have no access to it. You can delete your favorites by unselecting them in the app, clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

4. No account, no analytics of our own

No registration is required. We do not use any analytics or tracking services ourselves. Data processing by the advertising service is described in the next section.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ad banners from the Google AdMob service. The provider for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Ads are only loaded when there is an internet connection. The app checks locally on your device whether a connection exists. No data is sent to us in the process.

When an ad is loaded and displayed, Google may process data such as your IP address, your device’s advertising ID, device and software information (such as device model, operating system and language) and information about which ads were displayed or clicked. Google may use this data to select and measure ads. The data may also be transferred to countries outside the EU, such as the USA. We have no access to this data. We only receive aggregated advertising statistics from Google, for example on revenue and ad impressions.

Where consent is required for this processing, it is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by sending us an email to the address above. When you first start the app, it asks for your consent via a dialog from Google. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app under “Info” → “Privacy settings (ads)”. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

For more information on how Google processes data, see https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Permissions

The app does not access your device’s location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. It needs access to the internet and the network status in order to load ads and to check whether a connection exists. On Android, your device’s advertising ID may also be used for advertising.

7. App stores

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We have no influence on this.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we do not store any data about you ourselves, these rights have little practical effect with regard to us. For data that Google processes in connection with advertising, you can contact Google. If you have any questions, you can reach us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If the app gets new features that process data, we will update this policy accordingly.
